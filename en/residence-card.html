Photo for a Polish residence card (karta pobytu)

The photo for a Polish residence card application must meet the same requirements as a photo for a Polish ID card: 35×45 mm, light and uniform background, face straight to the camera, neutral expression, taken no earlier than 6 months before the application.

Take a photo with your phone and PhotoIDCreator will crop it to the required size, remove the background and place it on a 10×15 cm sheet that you can print at any photo print shop.
